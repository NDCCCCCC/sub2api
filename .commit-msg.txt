feat(ui): merge CN provider quota + balance cells into one usage cell

The account list showed two separate "查询" buttons for one supplier account
(coding-plan quota windows and payg balance). Users read that as two probes
and each click spent an extra upstream request.

- New CNProviderUsageCell renders quota tiers and balance in one cell with a
  single query button that fires both endpoints in parallel
  (Promise.allSettled), so one failed side keeps the other side's fresh data
  and only reports its own error
- Quota-only accounts (zhipu/kimi coding, opencode, command code, opendesign)
  and balance-only accounts (kimi/deepseek payg) still render just their
  applicable part
- Snapshot-staleness auto-probe merges both timestamps; the shared
  probe coalescing/debounce behaviour is preserved
- Deletes CNProviderQuotaCell.vue / CNProviderBalanceCell.vue and folds their
  specs into CNProviderUsageCell.spec.ts (11 cases covering both sides)
